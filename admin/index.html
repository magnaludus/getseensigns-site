1. New order and proof

A catalog item is sku x quantity (like banner-13oz-3x6 x 2) and uses the saved Stripe price. A custom line is Name | quantity | price in dollars. A line named Shipping adds a shipping charge.

2. Send the proof

Orders from the website start as awaiting_proof (shown as "needs proof" below). Click the order in the list, paste the link to the proof image, and the customer gets the proof email with their approval link. The same form sends a new proof after a revision request.
