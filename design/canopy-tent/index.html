Design your canopy tent. We'll proof it.

Pick a layout, add your name and logo, and turn your 10×10 tent around in 3D before you send it. Free design proof within 12 hours, 7 days a week.

Loading the designer…

What you can design

Your tent has a roof peak and a valance on all four sides. Put the same design on every side, or choose Each side different and design the front, right, back and left one at a time. Pick top and valance, top with back wall, or the full kit. The designer covers the printed top and valance. Walls, the flag and the table throw in the full kit are laid out on your free proof.

How the designer works

Start from one of four layouts, then change the words, colors and font, and add your logo as a PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG. Drag, resize and rotate anything on the flat view, then switch to 3D and walk around the tent to check every side. Guides show the roof shape, the valance line and a safe zone so your name does not land where it could be trimmed.

What it costs

Tents start at $649 with free shipping in the lower 48. The price you see in the designer is the price for the package you choose, and it matches our Etsy listing.

What happens next

Send your design and we email a free proof within 12 hours, 7 days a week, with 3 revisions included. Nothing prints until you approve it. The preview is not the print file: we build that on your proof. Tents arrive 5 to 10 business days after you approve.

FAQ

About the designer

Can each side of the tent be different?

Yes. Choose Each side different in the Sides tab, then design the front, right, back and left sides one at a time. You can also copy one side onto the others.

Is the preview the file you print?

No. It shows your words, colors and logo on the tent. We build the real print file during your free proof, and nothing prints until you approve it.

Do I have to upload a logo?

No. Start with words and colors, and tell us what you have when you send your proof request.
