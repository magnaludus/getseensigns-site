FAQ

About the preview

Is this preview the file you print?

No. It shows your words, colors and logo on the product. We build the real print file during your free proof. Nothing prints until you approve.

How fast is the free proof?

You get a free digital proof within 12 hours, 7 days a week, with 3 revisions included.

Which products can I design here?

A 10×10 canopy tent (front valance, same on all 4 sides or different text on each), feather flags in 10.5, 14 and 18 ft, 18×24 yard signs sold in packs of 5 or more, 6 ft and 8 ft table throws, and vinyl banners in the sizes we sell.

What do you need from me?

Your name, email and phone. Add a logo if you have one. We use what you send only to make your proof.
