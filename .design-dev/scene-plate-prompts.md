# Photo plates for the 3D scenes

Image prompts for the main assistant's free image tool. Claude does not make images with paid tools.
Each plate is the empty background the designer turns a product in front of. The designer draws a
shadow on the ground itself, so the photo must not contain the product, a sign, a tent or a flag.

Save each result as `assets/design/scenes/<name>-2048.webp` (long edge exactly 2048 px) and
`<name>-1024.webp` (long edge 1024 px). 3:2 landscape, WebP, sRGB, no transparency. Add one line to
`assets/design/scenes/CREDITS.md` ("yard: generated by Get Seen Signs"), set the scene's `"plate"` in
`src/design_catalog.json` to the name, and rebuild. See `src/README.md`, "Scenes".

## What every plate needs

- Style: a real, candid smartphone photo. Natural color, mid-bright exposure, no filters, no HDR glow.
- Camera: held about 5 ft (chest height), about a 40 mm lens, pointing a few degrees down so the horizon
  sits about 30 percent from the top of the frame. Shallow depth of field: the background is softly blurred.
- The middle matters most. On a phone only the middle third of the width is shown, so keep everything
  important there. The lower 45 percent of the frame and the middle 60 percent of its width must be flat,
  empty ground: no cars, people, steps, curbs, hard shadows or strong lines running to a vanishing point.
- Light: soft overcast or open shade. No hard sun shadows. No sun flare.
- None of these anywhere: people, vehicles, animals, readable text, numbers, logos, brand names, team
  names, flags, mascots or trademarks. Windows and walls are blank.

Paste this first, then the scene text:

> A real, candid smartphone photo, landscape 3:2, taken at chest height with a 40 mm lens tilted slightly
> down so the horizon is about 30 percent from the top. Soft overcast light, natural color, shallow depth
> of field with a blurred background. The lower half of the picture and the center of the picture are
> flat, empty, open ground with nothing on it. No people, no vehicles, no animals, no text, no numbers, no
> logos, no brand names, no signs, no flags.

## Phase 1

**yard** (front yard, for yard signs)

> A suburban front yard. Short, evenly mown green lawn fills the lower half and the center of the frame.
> About 30 feet behind it stands an ordinary one-story house with a plain front door and no house numbers,
> softly blurred. A few leafy trees at the far left and far right edges, and a strip of sidewalk far in the
> back. Nothing on the lawn.

**storefront** (strip-mall front, for feather flags and banners)

> A flat, clean concrete sidewalk in the foreground and center. About 10 to 15 feet behind it, the front of
> a generic one-story strip-mall store, softly blurred: large plain glass windows with no lettering, a solid
> color wall, a plain awning with no writing. Nothing on the sidewalk. The edges of the sidewalk run left
> and right, not toward a vanishing point.

## Phase 2

**range** (outdoor event area, for tents and flags)

> A flat gravel lot at an outdoor shooting-range event area in daylight, with low grass-covered earth
> berms and a plain fence far behind, softly blurred. No buildings with signs, no targets, no weapons, no
> vehicles. Open, empty gravel in the foreground and center.

**gunshow** (indoor expo hall, for tents, throws and flags)

> A large indoor expo hall seen from the aisle at eye level. Plain gray carpet in the foreground and
> center, empty. Far behind it, a blurred row of booths with white pipe-and-drape and bare tables. No
> signs, no banners, no products, no people.

**ballfield** (youth ballfield, for banners and tents)

> The outfield fence of a youth baseball field in daylight, plain dark green fence, with empty metal
> bleachers far to one side and short, even grass in the foreground and center, all softly blurred behind
> the grass. No scoreboard text, no ads on the fence, no team colors, no people.
