Which material?

13 oz standard is our everyday banner: scrim-reinforced vinyl for indoor and outdoor use, great for events, storefronts and parties. 18 oz heavy duty is our heaviest vinyl, a thicker blockout material for long-term outdoor jobs and rougher conditions. Mesh wind-through is perforated vinyl that lets wind pass through, made for fences, scaffolds and construction sites. Mesh lets some light through, so colors look a little less solid up close.

Which size?

In 13 oz, 2×4 ft is $44, 3×6 ft is $59, 4×8 ft is $89 and 3×10 ft is $79. In 18 oz heavy duty or mesh, 2×4 ft is $54, 3×6 ft is $84, 4×8 ft is $149 and 3×10 ft is $119. 2×4 ft fits a table front, a doorway or a small fence panel. 3×6 ft is the go-to for storefronts and "Now Open" banners. 4×8 ft and 3×10 ft are for building faces, long fences and anywhere people read from across a parking lot.

Grommets or pole pockets?

Grommets are for tying or zip-tying the banner to a fence, railing or wall, and sit about every 2 ft. Pole pockets are sleeves at the top and bottom for hanging on poles or a banner stand. Pole pockets are available on 13 oz and 18 oz banners at the same price. Mesh banners come with grommets.

FAQ

Vinyl Banner questions

Can I hang a vinyl banner outside year-round?

13 oz and 18 oz vinyl are made for outdoor use, and 18 oz is the pick for long-term jobs. Wind is the bigger risk than rain. Tie off every grommet so the banner can't flap and tear. On a fence or scaffold, mesh lets wind pass through.

Which banner is best for a chain-link fence?

Mesh wind-through is made for fences, scaffolds and construction sites. Zip-tie or bungee it straight through the grommets. 13 oz and 18 oz work on a fence too, as long as you tie off every grommet.

Do you make custom sizes?

The Etsy listing has 2×4, 3×6, 4×8 and 3×10 ft in each material. For another size, send a quote request with the dimensions.

Is the banner printed on both sides?

No. Banners are printed on one side.
