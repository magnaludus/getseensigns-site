# Shipping and returns

This page summarizes sections 3, 6 and 7 of our [Terms of Service](/terms). If anything here differs from the Terms, the Terms apply.

## How an order works
- You send your words, logo or design. We email a **free digital proof**, with 3 revisions included.
- **Nothing goes to production until you approve the proof and pay.** You pay from a private link in your proof email, with Stripe.
- Every product is custom made for you by our US trade printers and shipped directly from them. Because each item is made to your specs, it can't be resold.

## Shipping
- Most orders arrive in 3 to 8 business days after you approve and pay for your proof. Some items list a longer time on their product page, and the time on the product page applies to that item.
- Production times and arrival dates are estimates, not guarantees. Delays by carriers, weather, or suppliers are outside our control. If you need your order by a certain date, tell us before you approve and we'll tell you if it's realistic.
- Orders ship directly from our printers in plain packaging, with tracking, by the carrier the printer uses. That may be FedEx, UPS, USPS, or freight for large items.
- **Free shipping applies to the contiguous US (the lower 48 states).** For Alaska, Hawaii, US territories and APO/FPO addresses, ask us first. Shipping there may cost extra or may not be available.
- Check your shipping address before you pay. If a package is lost because of an address you gave us, reshipping costs are on you.
- Freight deliveries are usually curbside, and someone may need to be there to receive them. Check the shipment before you sign for it and note any damage on the delivery receipt.

## Cancellations and returns
- **Before you approve and pay:** cancel anytime. Reply to your proof email or ignore the link. You owe nothing.
- **After you approve and pay:** no cancellations, returns or refunds, because your custom item has gone to production.
- **Exceptions:** if your order arrives with a production defect or shipping damage, we reprint it at no cost. A defect means the product doesn't match the approved proof (allowing for normal color variation) or is physically flawed.
- Errors in the approved proof (spelling, wording, sizing and the like) are not defects, so please check your proof carefully.

## If something is wrong
Email sales@getseensignsco.com within **7 days of delivery** with your order number and clear photos of the problem and the packaging. We may ask you to keep the item until the claim is closed. Claims after 7 days aren't covered. Our remedy for a valid claim is a reprint. If a reprint isn't possible, we refund what you paid for the affected items.

If tracking shows a package as delivered but you can't find it, contact the carrier first, then email us within 7 days. We'll help with a carrier claim.
