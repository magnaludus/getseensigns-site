# About Get Seen Signs

Get Seen Signs is a veteran-owned, family-run online sign shop. We make custom yard signs, banners, feather flags, retractable banners, tents, table throws, backdrops and more for businesses, schools, teams, churches and families.

## How we work
- **Online only.** There's no storefront. You order online, and everything ships free to your door in the lower 48 states.
- **Made to order.** Every product is custom made for you by our US trade printers and shipped directly from them.
- **Proof first, then pay.** You get a free digital proof, with 3 revisions included. Nothing is printed or charged until you approve it and pay.
- **Design included.** Send your words and logo, or use our [online designer](/design/) to see it first.
