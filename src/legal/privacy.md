# Privacy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Get Seen Signs is based in Smiths Station, Alabama. This page explains, in plain terms, what we do with information you share with us.

## What we collect

- **What you send us:** your name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing address, and the details, artwork, logos, photos, or design previews you include in a quote request, a design request, an order, an email, or a text.
- **When you check out:** your order details and payment record. Payments on this site are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
- **Automatically:** our host and Cloudflare keep standard logs (like IP address and browser type) for security. If we turn on ad and analytics tools (see below), they also record how you use the site, like pages viewed and how you got here.

We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

## How we use it

- To reply to you, quote your job, and design your proof.
- To take payment and produce and ship your order. Your design and shipping details are shared with our print and shipping partners only for that purpose.
- To keep records of orders, proof approvals, and delivery, including to respond to payment disputes.
- To send our email newsletter, only if you sign up.
- To measure our website and ads, if we turn on the tools below.
- To keep basic business and tax records required by law.

## Payments

Checkout on this site runs through Stripe. Stripe processes your card and handles it under its own privacy policy. We get your name, email, address, what you bought, and the last four digits and type of card, not the full number.

## Email and newsletter

We send order and proof emails, and our newsletter if you opt in, through Brevo. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter anytime with the link in any email. You'll still get emails about your own orders.

## Ads and analytics

Right now this site doesn't run advertising trackers. We plan to add Google Analytics 4 and the Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Pixel to measure traffic and show our ads to people who visited. When we do, we'll update this page first. Those tools use cookies to record visits and actions on the site and share that data with Google and Meta. You'll be able to block cookies in your browser, opt out of Google Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout, and change your ad settings in your Meta account.

## Orders on Etsy

Orders placed through our Etsy shop are also covered by Etsy's privacy policy. Etsy handles payment for those orders; we never see your full card details.

## Your artwork

Files you send stay yours. We use them only for your order and don't post your design publicly without your permission. We may keep your files to make reorders easier. Ask us and we'll delete them unless we need them for an open order or dispute.

## Third-party services

This site serves its own fonts (no third-party font requests). We use these services to run the business, and each gets only what it needs: Cloudflare (site security and performance), our web host (server logs), Stripe (payments), Brevo (email), our print and shipping partners (your order), and, once added, Google and Meta (analytics and ads).

## How long we keep it

We keep order, payment, and proof records as long as we need them for business, tax, and legal reasons, generally up to 7 years.

## Children

This site isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Your choices

Want a copy of your information, or want us to fix or delete it? Email sales@getseensignsco.com and we'll take care of it. We may need to confirm it's you, and we may have to keep some records the law requires.
